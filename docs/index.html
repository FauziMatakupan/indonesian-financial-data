Cakupan penuh

The complete dataset adds all 4.408 rows, quarterly data (16.138 rows, 20 periods), 11 per-sector files, a codebook, and runnable R/Stata/SPSS syntax. It is available through the Fastwork listing.

Sampel dan halaman ini gratis; panel penuh berbayar.

Sebelum dipakai

  • Most observations are Q3 (30 September) unaudited, as published by IDX.
  • 2021–2022 ratios are preliminary and normalized ×100.
  • 2025 is a trailing year at snapshot time.
  • Blank cells mean the source published nothing — never zero.
  • Descriptive data only. Not investment advice.
